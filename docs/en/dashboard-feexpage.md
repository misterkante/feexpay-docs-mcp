# FeexPage

FeexPage is a feature that lets you create a web page for your products, services, events, etc.

### To do this:

1\. Click the **FeexPage** link in the menu.

2\. Click the **Create a page** button.

![Create a page](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/feexpage_new.png)

3\. Choose the template type that suits you.

![Template choice](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/modelepage_new.png)

4\. Fill out the form correctly.

-   Give your page a title.
-   Provide a description of your product.
-   Add an image.
-   If you want to set a fixed price, check **I want a fixed amount on this page**.
-   If you want to collect phone numbers, check **Collect phone numbers on this page**.
-   Customize your page link by filling in the custom link field.
-   Provide a success message.
-   Provide a return link after payment (for example, your website link).

![Creation form](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/form_feexpage_new.png)

5\. Click **Create**.

6\. In the list of pages displayed, click the eye icon for your page.

![Pages list](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/page_non_publiee_new.png)

7\. Click **Publish** to publish your page.

![Publish button](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/page_non_publiee_new.png)

Your page is now accessible to your customers.

-   View your page by clicking the **eye** button.
-   You can copy the link or share it by clicking **share** and selecting a sharing network.
-   You can also view payments made on your page by scrolling down.
-   You can download the payment list by clicking the **Download CSV** button.
-   You can unpublish the page by clicking **Switch to Draft mode**.

![Page actions](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexpage/fonctions_pages_new.png)
