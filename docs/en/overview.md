# Welcome to FeexPay!

FeexPay is a payment aggregator that lets you integrate payment methods into your applications. To get started, you need an account. If you don’t have one, you can [create it here](https://app-v2.feexpay.me/signup/)

Tip

FeexPay lets you accept payments in just a few minutes. Follow our quick start guide to get started now!

## Overview

FeexPay is a digital financial services platform designed to provide payment solutions tailored to the needs of professionals, businesses of all sizes, online stores, as well as public and non-profit organizations.

With a user-friendly interface and fast technical onboarding, FeexPay enables both online payment collection and fund transfers to users’ Mobile Money wallets. The system ensures secure transaction processing and provides practical tools to track and manage cash flows with ease.

### Who is FeexPay for?

#### Developers

Full RESTful API, multi-language SDKs, detailed technical documentation, and a sandbox testing environment.

#### E-commerce

Quick integration with major platforms, customizable checkout, and automated order management.

#### Businesses

Automated invoicing, analytics dashboards, multi-user management, and dedicated support.

#### NGOs & Government

Fundraising solutions, full traceability, detailed reporting, and regulatory compliance.
