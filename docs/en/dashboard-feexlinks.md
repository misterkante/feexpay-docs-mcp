# FeexLink

FeexLink is a feature that lets you create payment links for your products or services.

To use it, just follow these steps.

1\. Click the **FeexLink** menu in the dashboard sidebar.

2\. Click **Create a new link**.

![Create a new link](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexlinks/feexlink_annote_new.png)

3\. Fill out the form with the price and description of your product or service.

4\. Click **Next**.

![Price and description form](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexlinks/feexlink_form1_new.png)

5\. Fill out the next form properly.

6\. Click **Next**.

![Details form](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexlinks/feexlink_form_2_new.png)

7\. Choose an image file or logo for the product/service.

8\. Click **Next**.

![Upload image](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexlinks/feexlink_form3_new.png)

You have just created a payment link. Click the **eye** button to view details, or click **Copy** to copy the link. You can view your link and delete it using the action menu.

![Link actions](https://docs.feexpay.me/feexlinks/actions_feexlink_new.png)
