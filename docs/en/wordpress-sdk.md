# WordPress (WooCommerce)

[Download the WordPress plugin](https://wordpress.org/plugins/feexpay/). It is designed to make FeexPay easier to use on your WooCommerce site. Here are a few steps to configure FeexPay on your WordPress site.

### 1\. Download the plugin

[Download here](https://wordpress.org/plugins/feexpay/) and install the plugin on your WordPress site. Make sure you have installed and activated **WooCommerce** beforehand.

### 2\. Upload and activate your plugin

1.  Configure your plugin.
2.  Click **Settings**. You can then configure FeexPay using this form. Check **Enable Feexpay Payment** to activate FeexPay on your site. Fill in the form according to your preferences with a title and a description. You also need to provide your **API key** and your **shop id**.
