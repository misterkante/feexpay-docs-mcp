# Create an account

Before performing any operations with FeexPay, you must have a validated account.

### New account

Creating a new account is very easy.

1.  Click the following link: [Create a new account](https://app-v2.feexpay.me/signup/)
2.  Click the **Start** button

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/create_account/demarer_new.png)

3.Fill in the form with your first name, last name, email address, and company name.

4.Then click the **Sign up** button.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/create_account/form_compte_1_new.png)

5.Check your inbox to validate your registration. Click the validation link.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/create_account/mail_valider_new.png)

6.Fill in the form with your password.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/create_account/password_valider_new.png)

8.Check the box **I accept the terms and conditions** to accept the Terms of Use.

9.Click the **Finish** button.
