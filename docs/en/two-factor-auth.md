# Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)

One more step at login to protect your account

Two-factor authentication adds a step to the login: after your password, you enter a 6-digit code shown by an app on your phone. Even if someone gets your password, they cannot log in without your phone.

FeexPay does not send codes by SMS or email: the code is generated by your phone's app, even without an internet connection.

### Before you start

-   A smartphone with an authenticator app: **Google Authenticator**, **Microsoft Authenticator** or **Authy** (free on the App Store and Google Play).
-   The password of your FeexPay account.
-   A safe place to store your recovery codes (password manager, printed document kept out of reach).

### Enable two-factor authentication

1.  Log in to your FeexPay dashboard.
2.  Open your **Profile** from the menu in the top right corner, then find the **Security** block. You can also go through **Settings** → **Manage in my profile**.
3.  In the **Two-factor authentication** row, click **Enable**.

![Security block with the Enable button](https://docs.feexpay.me/two-factor/security_enable.png)

#### Step 1 of 4: confirm your password

Enter your password, then click **Continue**. The eye icon shows what you are typing.

#### Step 2 of 4: scan the QR code

1.  Open your authenticator app on your phone.
2.  Choose **Add an account** (often a **+** button), then **Scan a QR code**.
3.  Point your camera at the QR code displayed on the screen. A **FeexPay** account appears in the app, with a 6-digit code that changes every 30 seconds.
4.  Click **I have scanned the code**.

Can't scan? Under the QR code, click **Copy** next to the key, then choose **Enter a key** in the app and paste it.

![QR code and manual entry key](https://docs.feexpay.me/two-factor/qr_setup.svg)

#### Step 3 of 4: enter the first code

Type the 6-digit code shown by the app. It is validated automatically on the 6th digit.

If the code is rejected, wait for the next code (it changes every 30 seconds) and make sure your phone's time is set automatically.

#### Step 4 of 4: store your recovery codes

FeexPay displays **10 recovery codes**. They let you log in if you lose your phone.

-   Save them using **Copy**, **Download (.txt)** or **Print**.
-   Each code can only be used once.
-   **They will never be displayed again** after the window is closed.

Check **I have stored my codes safely**, then click **Finish**.

![List of recovery codes](https://docs.feexpay.me/two-factor/recovery_codes.svg)

Two-factor authentication is now enabled and a confirmation email is sent to you.

Warning

If you close the window before step 3, nothing is enabled. Simply start again from **Enable**.

### Log in with two-factor authentication

1.  Enter your email and password as usual.
2.  The **Two-step verification** screen appears: enter the 6-digit code from your app.
3.  On your usual computer, check **Remember this device for 30 days** so you don't have to enter a code on this browser for 30 days. Never check it on a shared computer.

![Two-step verification screen](https://docs.feexpay.me/two-factor/two_step_verification.svg)

After 5 wrong codes, input is blocked for 15 minutes. A countdown shows when to try again.

### You no longer have your phone

On the verification screen, click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your codes, with or without dashes. Once logged in, generate new codes.

Phone lost and no recovery codes left? Contact FeexPay support. To protect your account, support will verify your identity before resetting two-factor authentication.

### Manage two-factor authentication

Everything is done in **Profile** → **Security** → **Two-factor authentication**.

-   **Regenerate codes** creates 10 new codes. The old ones stop working immediately. Do this when you have 3 or fewer left.
-   **Trusted devices** the list of browsers exempt from the code. **Revoke all devices** forces everyone, including this one, to enter a code on the next login. Do this if you lost a computer or checked the box on a shared machine.
-   **Disable** requires your password and a code. Your account will then be protected only by your password.

Good to know

FeexPay will **never** ask you for your code by phone, email or message. If someone asks you for it, do not give it: it is a fraud attempt.
