# Account verification

Account verification is an important step to use FeexPay services in production. It helps fight fraud and improves security regarding the merchant’s identity and activity. Please follow these steps:

1.  Log in to your **account**
2.  Click the **Verify my account** button.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/validate_account/valider_ou_sandbox.png)

3.Fill in the form with your information.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/validate_account/form_validation_1.png)

4.Click the **Next** button.

5.Select your company type and your business category.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/validate_account/selectionner_type_entreprise.png)

6.Fill in the administrative information required according to the company type you selected.

![Create an account](https://docs.feexpay.me/validate_account/formulaire_administratif.png)

7.Click the **Continue** button.

8.You will receive a confirmation message for your request.

![accountValidation.images.final\_step](https://docs.feexpay.me/validate_account/final_step.png)
