# Payouts

Welcome to the Payouts menu. You can initiate payouts of your funds and also view your payout history.

### To initiate a payout:

1\. Click **Initiate a payout**.

![Initiate a payout](https://docs.feexpay.me/reversement/reversement_dash_new.png)

2\. Fill out the opened form with your payout details.

3\. Click **Next**.

![Payout form](https://docs.feexpay.me/reversement/form_revers1_new.png)

4\. Choose the payout type: Mobile Money, bank account or cryptocurrency.

![Payout type selection](https://docs.feexpay.me/reversement/choice.png)

5\. Fill out the form, then start the payout.

![Payout form](https://docs.feexpay.me/reversement/send.png)

6\. For a Mobile Money payout, the number used is the one registered in the <strong>Configuration</strong> tab of the dashboard.

Where do I configure my numbers?

Payout numbers are no longer configured from your profile. They are grouped by country and by network in the [Configuration](https://docs.feexpay.me/?section=dashboard-configuration) tab of the dashboard.
