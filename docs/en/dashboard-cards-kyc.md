# Cards KYB

The **Cards KYB** tab allows you to fill in the necessary information to accept credit card payments on your store. This feature is essential to expand your payment methods and offer more flexibility to your customers.

### To enable credit card payments:

1.  Log in to your FeexPay dashboard
2.  Click on the **Cards KYB** menu
3.  Fill out the form with your personal and professional information
4.  Upload required documents (ID card, proof of address, etc.)
5.  Submit your request for validation
6.  Wait for validation confirmation by email

![Cards KYB form](https://docs.feexpay.me/dashboard/dashboard_new.png)

### Required documents:

-   Valid ID card (national card, passport or driver's license)
-   Recent proof of address (electricity, water, internet bill)
-   Legal documents for companies (statutes, commercial register)
-   Bank statement for account verification
-   Recent photo of the account holder (for verification)

### Accepted card types:

![VISA](https://docs.feexpay.me/card/brands/visa.svg)

VISA debit and credit cards with 3DS

![MASTERCARD](https://docs.feexpay.me/card/brands/mastercard.svg)

Mastercard debit and credit cards with 3DS

#### Important:

-   The validation process can take 2-5 business days
-   All information must be accurate and up to date
-   Documents must be clear and readable
-   Once validated, you cannot modify certain information

### Benefits of activation:

-   Increase your conversion rates with more payment methods
-   Accept international payments
-   Enhanced security with 3D Secure technology
-   Real-time transaction processing
-   Detailed tracking of card transactions in your dashboard
